Read The Label

You're standing in the aisle holding a box that says "natural," "multigrain," and "made with real fruit," and you have no idea if it's good or not. That's on purpose. The front of the package is an ad. The back is the only part that legally has to tell you the truth.

This is the same scan I run on everything that goes in my cart. Five checks, in order, about ten seconds a package. No tracking app, no nutrition degree, no standing in the aisle for ten minutes while your kid melts down next to you.
