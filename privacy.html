Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Father Built Fitness respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you visit this website, submit a form, book a call, or participate in coaching.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, social-media handle, goals, and any answers you give on a booking or application form.

Booking forms may ask about your health, energy, and fitness goals so the call can be useful to you. You are never required to answer beyond what's needed to book.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically through the website, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage data.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and bookings;
  • Communicate with you about coaching, scheduling, and services;
  • Personalize your coaching plan;
  • Improve the website and communications;
  • Maintain business and administrative records; and
  • Protect the security of the website and services.

Third-Party Tools

Father Built Fitness may use third-party platforms such as a coaching app, a calendar and booking tool, email providers, payment processors, and website analytics. Information submitted through those services may also be governed by their own privacy policies.

Text Messages and Marketing Communications

If you provide your phone number or email, you may receive messages related to your booking, coaching, or service updates. You may opt out of promotional communications at any time by contacting Fatherbuiltfitness@gmail.com.

How We Share Information

Father Built Fitness does not sell your personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to operate the business, provide services, use trusted service providers, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of Father Built Fitness or its clients.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information submitted through the website and services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Father Built Fitness services and this website are intended for adults age 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information, contact Fatherbuiltfitness@gmail.com.

Your Choices

You may request to update or delete personal information we hold about you by contacting Fatherbuiltfitness@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained for legal or business recordkeeping.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Father Built Fitness at Fatherbuiltfitness@gmail.com.
