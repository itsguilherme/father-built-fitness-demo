Meet Your Coach

Hi, I'm Shaun

I got my diploma in personal training right out of high school, and since then I've worked as an in-person trainer, an online coach, and a paramedic.

I was the fat kid who didn't know he had a choice. Then I figured it out. Got into MMA, got in the best shape of my life. Felt untouchable. Then I lost the plot entirely.

Wrong relationships. Drinking. Drugs. Six to eight years where fitness didn't exist in my life. A friend looked at me one day and said something I'll never repeat in full, but the message was clear. I wasn't okay. And I already knew.

Then my ex cleared the accounts and left state with another man.

I took my son and I left.

I break down when I talk about the years before that. Because I was a bad dad. Not absent, but not there. Not really. And that's time I can never get back.

The day I got full custody was the day everything changed.

I swore I would not waste another second.

Getting back into shape was one of the hardest things I've ever done. Not physically, emotionally. I knew where I'd been. I knew how far I'd fallen. I avoided the gym because I was embarrassed by the gap between who I was and who I used to be.

But I showed up anyway.

Not because I suddenly had discipline. Not because I found the perfect programme. Because I had a reason that was bigger than the excuses.

My son was watching.

I coach men now because I've been the man who had it all physically and threw it away. I've been the man who hated himself in the mirror. I've been the man who couldn't justify spending an hour on himself because the guilt was too heavy.

I know what that feels like. Which means I know what it takes to get out of it.

If you're somewhere in this story, this is for you.

You don't have a discipline problem. You don't have a time problem.

You have a reason problem. And when you find the right one, everything changes.
